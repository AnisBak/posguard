# posguard v0.2.0 (2026-11-15)

This release adds **EBT readiness**, the project's new focus. It also includes Milestone 2 (relay-risk flags and the webhook sink). It is still an early release for evaluation and feedback. It isn't on Maven Central yet, and APIs may change before 1.0.

posguard is an open-source Android toolkit that protects the **merchant app around a certified payment engine**. It never replaces the engine, never touches card data or PINs, and makes no PCI claim.

## Why EBT

On October 23, 2026, USDA proposed that SNAP retailers accept **chip and contactless (tap) EBT cards**, with magnetic-stripe fallback only after a failed chip attempt ("Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program: Electronic Benefits Transfer Card Security Measures", RIN 0584-AE99, 91 FR 66958; comments due December 22, 2026). USDA reports at least $360 million in stolen SNAP benefits replaced through mid-fiscal-year 2025. Retailers pay for their own POS equipment, and small retailers' Android POS apps need EBT checkout flows that are safe and reliable during outages. The roadmap was re-prioritized around this (CHANGELOG, ADR-003).

## What's new

| Area | What it does |
|---|---|
| `posguard-ebt` (new) | Splits the basket into SNAP-eligible and other items using config, with no eligibility list shipped and unknown categories treated as not eligible. Plans split tender and moves a partial-approval shortfall to the next tender. Card entry is chip first, with magstripe only after a failed chip attempt and never key entry. Each EBT payment goes through the outage-safe ledger. The receipt has the proposed minimum fields, and balances are kept off the POS screen. Card reading and PIN entry stay inside the certified engine (`EbtEngine` adapter). |
| `ebt-simulator` (new, **not real**) | Fictional magstripe-only, chip, and chip-and-contactless cards with fictional balances. Covers partial approvals, empty balances, a chip read failure, a reader without the SNAP chip app, and a network drop after submit. |
| Ledger | Records partial approvals (`approvedAmount`, schema v3, non-destructive migration). `combinedStatusSource` recovers payments sent to different engines (EBT and card) without ever voiding a charged payment. |
| Relay-risk flags (experimental) | `RelayRiskMonitor` applies pluggable rules to engine-reported tap metadata (for example, a burst of phone-wallet taps). It holds approved payments for the merchant's review, and preflight warns until reviewed. It never declines. |
| Webhook sink | `WebhookSink` posts scrubbed event batches over HTTPS with optional HMAC-SHA256 signing. It never follows redirects. |
| Telemetry | New event types `RELAY_RISK_FLAGGED`, `RELAY_RISK_REVIEWED`, `EBT_ENTRY_FALLBACK`, and `EBT_PARTIAL_APPROVAL`. None carry amounts or balances. |
| Demo | Scenario 9: an EBT purchase with SNAP split, chip-to-tap fallback, a partial approval, the rest by card, and a receipt. Extra controls cover a magstripe-only card, a reader without the SNAP chip app, and an empty balance. Scenario 7: a wallet-tap burst held for review. |

## Verification

- 298 unit and Robolectric tests, all passing. Lint is clean on the library modules.
- EBT tests cover the config loader, basket split, tender planning, every entry-flow transition, checkout through the real ledger (including a lost response recovered by key), the simulator, and the demo flow. A regression test confirms that an EBT payment lost after submit is recovered as approved, never voided.
- Scenarios 7 and 9 ran end to end on a Pixel 9 emulator (API 36), with a regression run of scenarios 1, 4, and 5 (one processor charge each). The README has the GIF and screenshots.
- The proposed-rule facts in `docs/ebt.md` were checked against the published Federal Register text, with quotes and page numbers.

## Breaking changes (pre-1.0)

- `EngineResult.Authorized`, `EngineStatus.Authorized`, and `TransactionRecord` have a new optional `approvedAmount`. Positional destructuring and `copy` calls may need updating.
- `GuardEvent` has new subtypes. Exhaustive `when` expressions need an `else` branch.
- The ledger database moves to schema v3 automatically. Existing rows are kept.

## Known limitations

- **EBT is simulator-only.** It has not been tested with a real EBT processor, a certified engine, or a state EBT specification. The eligibility config is the integrator's responsibility. The demo's config is for a fictional jurisdiction and is not legal guidance.
- **The proposed rule may change** before it becomes final. It sets no retailer acceptance deadline.
- **Relay-risk flags** are experimental, untuned, and see only this device's engine metadata. Expect false positives.
- Not yet verified on real devices. The v0.1.0 limitations on real overlay and recording checks and on the Play Integrity backend still apply.
